Privacy Policy

Vyllo — Personal media tracker

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Vyllo is a personal collection tracker for books, games, and films/series. The data controller is the entity operating Vyllo (contact: support@vyllo-app.com).

2. What data we collect

  • Your collection content: titles, status, progress, ratings, notes and completion dates.
  • Preferences and settings: theme, language, active categories, annual goals — stored locally on your device.
  • SteamID (optional), only if you choose to connect your Steam account. It is stored with your account so your Steam hours and achievements can sync, including in the background while the app is closed. Disconnecting Steam deletes it.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage data to improve the app, not linked to your identity.
  • If you use Vyllo Playtime for Windows: the games found on that PC and your play sessions, as described in section 6.

We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, or payment details within the app.

3. How and where we store your data

Your collection, settings, and goals are stored locally (on your device or the app's private server). We do not sell, share, or rent your personal data to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

4. Third-party services

To fetch covers, titles, and metadata, the app queries third-party services: TheTVDB, TVmaze, RAWG, Steam (Valve Corporation), Open Library, Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), and optionally TMDB. When you use AI search, an external service receives only the search term — never your account data. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Premium subscription

If you purchase Vyllo Premium, payment is processed by the app store (App Store / Google Play). Vyllo does not store credit card data. Subscription status is verified locally.

6. Vyllo Playtime for Windows

Vyllo Playtime is an optional Windows app, part of Vyllo Premium, that measures how long you play games on your PC and adds that time to your Vyllo collection. It does nothing until you link it to your account by approving a code in the Vyllo app.

What it reads on your PC. To find your games, it reads the installed-games lists of Steam, Epic Games and Battle.net, and the game folders you choose. To measure playtime, it checks which programs are running and only looks at those inside the folder of a game it tracks. It does not record other programs, websites, keystrokes, screenshots, or anything you do outside your games.

What it sends to Vyllo.

  • When you link the PC: the name you give the PC, the Windows version, and the app version.
  • To identify a game: the launcher, the store's game ID (for example, the Steam AppID), and the game's name. For games you add yourself, only the name of the game's folder, the name of its executable, and the product name stored in that file.
  • Which of your identified games are installed on that PC, so the Vyllo app can show them.
  • For each play session: the game, when it started and ended, and why it ended (for example, the game was closed or the PC went to sleep).

It never sends full file paths, your Windows user name, your SteamID, the other programs installed or running on your PC, or the games you chose to ignore or remove.

What stays on your PC. Sessions are saved on the PC first, so no playtime is lost while you are offline, and are sent when there is a connection. Sent sessions are kept on the PC for 90 days and then deleted. Logs are kept for 7 days, without your personal folder names, and only leave the PC if you export them and send them to us yourself. The link to your account is stored in the Windows Credential Manager as a token that only works for Vyllo Playtime; there is no password on the PC.

Game covers. The app downloads game covers directly from IGDB (images.igdb.com, operated by Twitch), which, like any website, receives your IP address.

Your control. You can ignore or remove any game, and unlink the PC at any time from the Windows app. Unlinking revokes the PC's access immediately. Uninstalling the app deletes its data on the PC; sessions already sent stay in your Vyllo account until you delete them or your account.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, rectify, export, and delete your data. You can export your entire collection at Settings → Data & Cloud, and delete items individually at any time. For full deletion requests or other GDPR matters, contact support@vyllo-app.com.

8. Children

Vyllo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Cookies and tracking

The app does not use tracking cookies or behavioral advertising.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy. For material changes, we will notify you through the app. The revision date is shown at the top of this document.
