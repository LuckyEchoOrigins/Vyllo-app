    Vyllo Playtime · Premium

    A small Windows app that notices when a game opens and closes, however you launch it, and sends each session to the Vyllo app on your iPhone or Android phone.

    Example · Recent sessions in Vyllo

    Good to know before you try it

    • Windows 10 (version 2004) or 11 only.
    • Finds Steam, Epic Games, Battle.net, GOG and Ubisoft Connect games on its own. Xbox app (Game Pass) and EA app games aren't read automatically yet; many can be added by hand.
    • Part of Vyllo Premium, and it needs the Vyllo app on your phone to link the PC.
    • The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show a warning the first time.
